# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is executives and product leaders looking for hands-on AI and product consulting. Secondary audiences are conference organizers evaluating Freddy as a speaker and hiring leaders considering him for senior product leadership roles. They arrive with limited time and need to understand his point of view, see evidence that he builds what he advocates, and find a clear path to start a conversation on LinkedIn.

## Product Purpose

The site presents Freddy Montes as a product leader who closes the gap between strategy and execution by building real systems inside a working product organization. Success means a visitor absorbs that thesis, trusts the evidence behind it, and contacts Freddy about consulting, speaking, or recruiting.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, deliberate. The experience should feel clean and assured, with the considered proportion, functional clarity, and patient pacing associated with Braun watches. Visitors should feel that they have enough space and time to read, think, and absorb each idea.

## Anti-references

Do not radically replace the current identity or abandon its blue-gray palette. Avoid service-landing-page grammar such as stacked color bands, repeated label-heading-copy blocks, and project grids. Also avoid flashy parallax, rapid autoplay, decorative gradients, noisy maximalism, startup-style cards, and motion that competes with the writing. Do not invent project screenshots or use generic placeholders while real visual evidence is unavailable.

## Design Principles

- Tell one cumulative story, not a compressed resume.
- Make each scroll moment inherit and transform what came before it.
- Give every major idea enough time and space to register.
- Demonstrate credibility through specific outcomes and working methods.
- Make restraint feel intentional through proportion, rhythm, and precision.
- Guide consulting, speaking, and recruiting interest toward one clear conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, keyboard access, semantics, and responsive behavior. Provide a complete reduced-motion experience that preserves the narrative without scroll-dependent animation. Keep reading comfortable through clear hierarchy, controlled line length, and sufficient pacing on both desktop and mobile.
