# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary audience is executive recruiters and founders seeking product and AI advisory help. They arrive with limited time and need to quickly understand Freddy's executive scope, technical depth, business judgment, and evidence of operating inside a real product organization.

## Product Purpose

The site positions Freddy Montes as a product executive who turns AI strategy into working systems. It should build confidence through a clear narrative, demonstrate the connection between strategy and execution, and lead qualified visitors to message Freddy on LinkedIn.

## Brand Personality

Decisive, technical, and business oriented. The voice is direct, specific, and grounded in shipped work rather than abstract claims.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense, cluttered interfaces, dashboard-like information overload, generic editorial portfolio styling, decorative technology motifs, and motion without narrative purpose.

## Design Principles

- Lead with a sharp point of view, then prove it with specific work.
- Make business impact and technical credibility legible in the same story.
- Use visual tension and pacing to reward scrolling without slowing comprehension.
- Prefer decisive hierarchy and whitespace over interface density.
- End every narrative beat by moving qualified visitors toward LinkedIn.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard accessibility. Preserve the full narrative and interaction model for reduced-motion users, replacing spatial motion with immediate state changes or restrained crossfades.
