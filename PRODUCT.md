# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, product and engineering leaders, conference organizers, and potential collaborators evaluating Freddy Montes. They arrive from a talk, a post, or a search, and skim in under a minute to answer one question: is this person the real thing?

## Product Purpose

fmontes.com is a personal positioning site for Freddy Montes, VP of Product at dotCMS. It argues one thesis: he builds the AI systems he leads teams to ship. Success = a visitor leaves able to repeat the thesis and knowing four concrete systems that back it up.

## Brand Personality

Direct, evidence-first, confident. The voice makes claims and immediately backs them with shipped work ("Strategy without prototypes is speculation", "I don't theorize. I run one."). Emotional goal: quiet conviction, not hype.

## Anti-references

- Buzzword thought-leader pages (leverage/transform/empower language).
- Generic SaaS-template landing pages: identical card grids, hero-metric blocks, gradient text.
- Resume-style portfolio listicles. The site is an argument, not a CV.

## Design Principles

1. **The scroll is the story.** The page is a narrative arc (thesis → beliefs → proof → stance), and pacing, white space, and motion exist to serve that arc.
2. **Show the work.** Every claim is adjacent to a shipped system. Nothing decorative that a skeptic could call vapor.
3. **Typography carries the design.** Editorial serif at display sizes, restrained palette; no imagery needed — the words are the product.
4. **Practice what you preach.** The site itself should feel like something a builder made: fast, precise, technically clean.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support: the story must read completely as a static page. All content server-rendered and readable without JavaScript. Dark mode via `prefers-color-scheme`.
