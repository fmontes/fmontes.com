# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, VPs, recruiters, conference organizers, and product/engineering leaders who land here from Freddy's talks, podcast, posts, or dotCMS work. They arrive mid-evaluation ("is this person the real deal on AI-native product orgs?") and scan fast. The job to be done: verify credibility in under a minute, then start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Personal site for Freddy Montes, VP of Product at dotCMS. It exists to prove one thesis: he builds the AI systems he leads teams to ship. Success is a qualified conversation started (LinkedIn / X / GitHub outreach), with scroll depth through the "Recently built" case studies as the supporting signal.

## Brand Personality

Practitioner, not pundit. Precise, warm, opinionated. Editorial voice: short declaratives, no buzzwords, receipts over claims. Three words: exact, builder, direct.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (hero-metric templates, gradient text, identical card grids)
- AI-slop editorial pastiche: eyebrow kickers above every section, numbered scaffolding everywhere
- "Thought leader" sites with stock imagery and aphorism-only copy
- Portfolio maximalism that drowns the words in WebGL spectacle

## Design Principles

1. The page performs its thesis: the site itself must feel like a precisely engineered system; motion is evidence, not decoration.
2. Words first: typography carries the design; every effect must serve legibility and cadence.
3. Contact is the climax: hierarchy and energy build toward starting a conversation.
4. Restraint with intent: one signature move per section beats a uniform reveal applied to everything.
5. Fast and honest: no heavy dependencies, content visible without JS, reduced motion respected everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text). Every animation ships with a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (instant or crossfade). Content never gated on JS: server-rendered HTML is fully readable without client hydration. Hit areas ≥ 40px on interactive elements.
